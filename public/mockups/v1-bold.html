Vishnu · Feed

A feed that respects your time.

A chronological stream of notes from people you follow — organized by channels like #tech and #random. No ranking algorithm deciding what you think about. Reply, quote, save, or share with one tap.

Brahma · Graph

Watch your knowledge connect.

Every note is a node; every reference you draw is an edge. Saved, own, and draft notes form a living map of what matters — pan it, add to it, and compose new notes right on the canvas.

Shiv · On-device AI

Ask your own notes anything.

Shiv is an AI assistant that runs on your phone — no cloud required. It answers from the notes you’ve created and saved, grounded in your own knowledge through GraphRAG, and branches any chat into a tree of follow-ups.
